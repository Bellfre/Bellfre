✅ Registry Direction

We clarified several foundational ideas:

The Clearinghouse is the product, not just a registry.
The Registry stands on its own; the SDK is simply one way to interact with it.
Identity and discovery are separate concerns.
Trust should be based on provenance, not arbitrary rankings.
Publisher-submitted records should be distinguished from indexed or referenced records.
💰 Business Model

The current thinking is converging on:

Free registration to maximize adoption.
Paid business features (verification, management, enterprise capabilities).
Discovery/API services as a long-term revenue stream.
Tomorrow's Goal

No code.

Phase 4 — Workflow

Walk through real scenarios:

A hobby developer registers an agent.
A company publishes its first commercial agent.
Bellfre discovers an agent on GitHub.
A publisher claims an indexed agent.
An agent is updated.
An agent is retired.

If every scenario has a clean, understandable flow, the implementation will follow naturally.
