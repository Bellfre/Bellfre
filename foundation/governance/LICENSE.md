# Bellfre License and Usage Framework

## Purpose

This document defines the licensing philosophy and usage expectations for the Bellfre ecosystem.

Bellfre is designed as an open infrastructure standard for agent interoperability, discovery, and governance.

The goal is to encourage adoption while protecting the integrity of the Bellfre identity, specifications, and ecosystem.

---

## Ownership

The Bellfre name, trademarks, specifications, reference implementations, and official infrastructure remain controlled by the Bellfre project and its governing authority.

Use of Bellfre technology does not transfer ownership of:

- Bellfre trademarks
- Bellfre standards
- Bellfre registries
- Bellfre governance processes
- Bellfre identity systems

---

## Permitted Use

Participants may:

- build Bellfre-compatible agents
- implement Bellfre-compatible capabilities
- integrate Bellfre standards into their own systems
- create applications using Bellfre technology

---

## Restricted Use

Participants may not:

- represent unofficial systems as official Bellfre services
- create competing Bellfre registries using Bellfre identity
- remove attribution or provenance requirements
- modify Bellfre specifications and represent them as official standards
- create confusion regarding Bellfre certification or governance

---

## Commercial Use

Commercial use of Bellfre-compatible technology is encouraged.

However, commercial implementations must preserve:

- interoperability
- transparency
- attribution
- BAT integrity
- BUI identity requirements

---

## Future Licensing

Specific software components may use separate licenses.

Specifications, trademarks, registries, and governance rules may have additional requirements.
