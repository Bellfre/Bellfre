# Bellfre Compliance Framework

## Purpose

The Bellfre Compliance Framework defines the requirements for systems, agents, services, and interfaces that claim compatibility with the Bellfre ecosystem.

Compliance exists to ensure that Bellfre-compatible implementations maintain the principles of:

    * interoperability
    * transparency
    * data integrity
    * trust
    * consistent behavior

Compliance is not intended to prevent innovation. It exists to ensure that implementations built on Bellfre preserve the value of the network.

---

# Compliance Levels

Bellfre compliance may evolve into multiple levels depending on implementation maturity.
Initial compliance categories:

## Bellfre Compatible

A system that follows published Bellfre technical specifications.
Requirements may include:

    * valid BAT handling
    * compatible capability descriptions
    * proper policy integration
    * adherence to published specifications

---

## Bellfre Verified

A system that has undergone additional review.
Requirements may include:

    * verified identity
    * documented ownership
    * security review
    * data integrity validation
    * operational transparency

---

## Bellfre Certified

A system recognized by the Bellfre Governance Committee as meeting established ecosystem standards.
Requirements may include:

    * full specification compliance
    * governance participation
    * audit capability
    * demonstrated trust practices

---

# Core Compliance Requirements

## 1. BAT Compliance

A compliant implementation must:

    * properly consume BAT records
    * preserve BAT identifiers
    * maintain record integrity
    * respect provenance information
    * support published BAT specifications

A compliant implementation must not:

    * alter official BAT records
    * create conflicting BAT identifiers
    * misrepresent non-BAT identifiers as BATs
    * remove verification information

---

# 2. Data Integrity Compliance

Bellfre-compatible systems must preserve the integrity of registered information.
Information may include:

    * capabilities
    * services
    * expertise
    * credentials
    * pricing
    * policies
    * reviews
    * interaction records

Implementations must maintain:

    * source attribution
    * timestamps
    * revision history
    * verification state

---

# 3. Policy Compliance

Agents participating in Bellfre should support policy-aware execution.
Policy systems should:

    * evaluate permitted actions
    * enforce restrictions
    * provide explainable decisions
    * prevent unauthorized capability use

Policy systems should not:

    * silently bypass restrictions
    * modify execution records
    * hide policy decisions from required interfaces

---

# 4. BUI Compliance

Interfaces presenting Bellfre information must:

    * accurately represent Bellfre relationships
    * display required identity information
    * preserve BAT information
    * maintain interoperability

A BUI implementation must not:

    * impersonate official Bellfre services
    * create misleading interfaces
    * obscure ownership
    * interfere with Bellfre systems

---

# 5. Security Compliance

Implementations should follow reasonable security practices including:

    * protection of credentials
    * secure data handling
    * access controls
    * audit logging where appropriate

Implementations must not intentionally:

    * disrupt Bellfre infrastructure
    * interfere with registries
    * degrade network operation
    * manipulate trust information

---

# 6. Quality and Authenticity Standards

Bellfre systems should maintain high-quality information standards.
Participants should avoid:

    * fabricated capabilities
    * false credentials
    * artificial reviews
    * misleading claims
    * automated low-quality records

The goal of Bellfre is trusted discovery, not volume of records.

---

# Compliance Testing

Future compliance testing may include:

    * specification validation
    * BAT verification
    * interface review
    * security assessment
    * data integrity checks
    * interoperability testing

Testing procedures will be defined and maintained by the Bellfre Governance Committee.

---

# Non-Compliance

Failure to meet Bellfre standards may result in:

    * removal of certification status
    * loss of verified designation
    * restrictions on official ecosystem participation

Non-compliant implementations may continue to operate independently but may not represent themselves as officially compliant.

---

# Governance and Updates

The Bellfre Governance Committee maintains this framework.
Updates should follow the Bellfre governance process:

    1. Proposal
    2. Review
    3. Community feedback
    4. Approval
    5. Published revision

Compliance requirements should evolve with:

    * technology changes
    * ecosystem needs
    * security requirements
    * interoperability improvements

---

# Guiding Principle

Bellfre compliance exists to protect trust.
The purpose of the framework is not to control what developers build.
The purpose is to ensure that when something identifies itself as Bellfre-compatible, users and systems can rely on what that means.
