# Bellfre Governance Council (BGC) Charter

**Version:** Draft 0.1

---

# Purpose

The Bellfre Governance Council (BGC) is the stewardship body responsible for guiding the long-term evolution of the Bellfre ecosystem.

The Council exists to preserve the integrity, interoperability, and trust of Bellfre while encouraging innovation and broad participation.

The BGC is not intended to manage day-to-day development or dictate how developers build their solutions.

Its role is to protect the principles upon which the Bellfre ecosystem is founded.

---

# Mission

The mission of the Bellfre Governance Council is to ensure that Bellfre remains:

* Open to innovation
* Stable in its standards
* Trusted by its participants
* Transparent in its governance
* Sustainable for future generations of developers and organizations

---

# Guiding Philosophy

The Council recognizes that successful ecosystems grow through participation.

Bellfre should encourage builders, researchers, organizations, and communities to contribute new ideas while preserving the shared standards that make interoperability possible.

The Council serves the ecosystem.

The ecosystem does not serve the Council.

---

# Areas of Stewardship

The BGC provides stewardship over the Bellfre Foundation, including:

* Technical specifications
* Bellfre Agent Tag (BAT) standards
* Bellfre Universal Interface (BUI) standards
* Governance policies
* Compliance framework
* Brand and identity guidance
* Long-term interoperability

Additional responsibilities may be defined as the Bellfre ecosystem evolves.

---

# Core Responsibilities

The Council may:

* Review and approve specification updates
* Publish new versions of Bellfre standards
* Maintain governance documentation
* Oversee compatibility guidance
* Establish certification criteria
* Resolve questions regarding official standards
* Encourage community participation

The Council should seek consensus whenever practical.

---

# What the Council Does Not Do

The Council does not:

* Control how developers build their own software
* Dictate implementation details beyond published standards
* Restrict innovation that remains compatible with Bellfre
* Operate as a commercial gatekeeper
* Replace individual project governance

The Bellfre ecosystem grows through compatible implementations, not centralized control.

---

# Membership

The composition of the Council is expected to evolve over time.

Future membership may include representatives from:

* Founding stewards
* Technical contributors
* Industry partners
* Academic researchers
* Enterprise adopters
* Independent developers
* Community representatives

The Council should strive for diverse experience and balanced representation.

---

# Working Groups

As Bellfre expands, the Council may establish working groups responsible for specific areas such as:

* Specifications
* Security
* Compliance
* Discovery
* Identity
* Developer Experience
* Documentation

Working groups report recommendations to the Council but do not independently redefine Bellfre standards.

---

# Decision Making

The Council should operate with the principles of:

* Transparency
* Technical merit
* Long-term thinking
* Ecosystem benefit
* Documented decisions

Whenever possible, significant changes should be published for community review before adoption.

---

# Community Participation

The Bellfre ecosystem is strengthened through community involvement.

Developers and organizations are encouraged to:

* Submit proposals
* Identify issues
* Recommend improvements
* Participate in discussions
* Share implementation experience

Constructive participation is considered an essential part of Bellfre's evolution.

---

# Evolution

This Charter is intended to evolve alongside the Bellfre ecosystem.

As the project matures, future versions may define:

* Membership processes
* Council terms
* Voting procedures
* Working group charters
* Proposal processes
* Appeals and dispute resolution
* Certification oversight

Early versions intentionally remain lightweight to encourage growth while establishing a clear direction.

---

# Founding Stewardship

Bellfre was established with the belief that AI agents require a trusted, interoperable infrastructure rather than isolated implementations.

During the formative stages of the project, the founder serves as the initial steward of the Bellfre vision and governance.

As the ecosystem matures, stewardship is expected to broaden through the Bellfre Governance Council and its participants, ensuring that Bellfre remains guided by its principles rather than any single individual or organization.

---

# Closing Principle

The Bellfre Governance Council exists to protect trust, not to accumulate authority.

Its success will be measured not by the number of rules it creates, but by the confidence developers, organizations, and users place in the Bellfre ecosystem.

Stewardship is a responsibility.

Trust is the outcome.
