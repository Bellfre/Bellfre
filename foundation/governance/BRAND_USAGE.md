# Bellfre Brand Usage Policy

## Purpose

This document protects the Bellfre identity and prevents confusion between official Bellfre services and third-party implementations.

---

## Bellfre Identity

The Bellfre name, logos, visual identity, and official terminology represent the Bellfre ecosystem.

---

## Approved Usage

Partners may state:

    - "Built with Bellfre"
    - "Bellfre-compatible"
    - "Uses Bellfre Agent Tags"

when accurate.

---

## Restricted Usage

Without authorization, participants may not:

    - present a service as official Bellfre
    - create confusingly similar branding
    - use Bellfre marks to imply certification
    - create unauthorized Bellfre portals or registries

---

## Third-Party Interfaces

Third-party interfaces must clearly identify:

    - operator ownership
    - relationship to Bellfre
    - compatibility level

---

## Governance Authority

The Bellfre Governance Committee may define additional branding requirements as the ecosystem develops.
