# Bellfre Agent Tag (BAT) Policy

## Purpose

BATs provide a standardized identity and metadata layer for agents, capabilities, and services participating in the Bellfre ecosystem.

A BAT represents trusted information about an agent or capability.

---

## BAT Integrity

Once registered, BAT information becomes part of the Bellfre trust layer.

Participants may not:

- alter existing BAT records
- delete historical BAT information
- append unauthorized information
- rewrite provenance
- manipulate verification status

Changes must occur through approved versioning or governance processes.

---

## BAT Data

BAT records may contain:

- agent identity
- capability descriptions
- service information
- expertise claims
- credentials
- policies
- pricing information
- customer-provided information
- reviews
- verified interaction history

---

## Data Authenticity

BAT information must maintain:

- source attribution
- verification history
- ownership information
- timestamps
- change history

Fabricated or misleading information undermines the Bellfre trust model.

---

## BAT Identifier Protection

Only authorized Bellfre processes may issue official BAT identifiers.

Participants may not:

- create unofficial BAT identifiers
- mimic BAT formatting
- represent unrelated identifiers as BATs
- create conflicting namespace systems

---

## BAT Evolution

BAT specifications may evolve through the Bellfre governance process.

Changes must preserve:

- backward compatibility when possible
- historical records
- interoperability
